I'm **Arpit Jain**, a Platform / DevOps engineer based in Bengaluru, India, with **3+ years** of experience running production infrastructure across **AWS, Kubernetes, OpenShift, and bare-metal** environments.

My work spans the full platform stack: infrastructure automation with **Terraform** and GitOps with **ArgoCD**, high-performance networking and workloads with **DPDK, SR-IOV, and Cilium**, distributed storage with **Ceph and Mayastor**, and multi-cluster management with **OpenShift ACM**. I build Go-based platform tooling and care a lot about doing infrastructure **securely** — I've driven CIS Level 2 server compliance from the 30–50% range up past 85% using OpenSCAP, Kyverno, Kubescape, and kube-bench.

## What I work on

- **Cloud & IaC** — multi-account AWS (EKS, EC2, Lambda, ECR, IAM, VPC, S3, SNS, SQS, ACM PCA) provisioned with reusable Terraform modules and approval-gated GitHub Actions pipelines.
- **Platform engineering & Kubernetes** — production vanilla Kubernetes and OpenShift clusters for high-performance workloads, integrating Cilium/eBPF, Ceph, Mayastor, KubeVirt, SR-IOV, and MetalLB.
- **GitOps & release automation** — ArgoCD + GitHub Actions with controlled rollout strategies, including blue/green and canary deployments.
- **Infrastructure security & compliance** — OpenSCAP, OVAL, Kyverno, Kubescape, kube-bench, Pod Security Standards, and custom node-audit tooling.
- **Observability** — Prometheus, Grafana, VictoriaMetrics, OpenTelemetry, Loki, CloudWatch.
- **Go-based tooling** — platform services and automation, including a DNS management platform integrating PowerDNS and Akamai with request-level metering.

## Background

I hold a **B.Tech from IIT Palakkad** (2019–2023). I currently work at **Nava** as a Platform / DevOps engineer, and previously spent ~2.5 years at **Jio Platforms** as an SRE / DevOps engineer operating OpenShift at telco scale in air-gapped environments.
